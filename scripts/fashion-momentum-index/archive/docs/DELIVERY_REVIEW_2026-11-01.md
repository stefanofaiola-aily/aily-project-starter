## Dinner list

The event CSV contains 32 nonempty brand rows, representing 31 unique brands.
All 31 are in the current input bundle and delivery and marked FMI required.
All 18 unique brands marked `Invited=Y` are included. The other 13 have unknown
invitation status, not a confirmed negative. Michael Kors appears twice and has
one delivery row. Aliases resolve Abercromie & Fitch to Abercrombie & Fitch,
The Gap to GAP Inc and Hermes to Hermès. GAP Inc remains a labelled group proxy;
event-name reconciliation does not approve standalone financial scope.

The delivery command writes `dinner-reconciliation.json` and fails if a dinner
brand is absent, not mandatory or has a mismatched invitation flag.

## Retrieved data versus released scores

The authenticated 1 November extraction contains 12,218 financial candidates
and 96,177 Instagram capture rows. Among the 94 prospects, 50 have revenue
candidates and 59 have Instagram captures. All 31 dinner brands have Instagram
captures and 22 have revenue candidates. This is queried source coverage, not
proof of standalone reported revenue or historical eligibility.

No dinner-brand revenue candidates were retrieved for Coach, Michael Kors,
The Row, Kate Spade, Rag & Bone, Uniqlo, Zara, H&M and Longchamp. Group revenue
must not silently replace brand revenue.

Zero mappings, analyses, peers and financial selections are approved. Of the 94
analyses, 79 have no route, 14 use show windows and one uses annual windows.
80 have missing handle/cutoff eligibility; the 14 show analyses have no eligible
timezone-qualified social captures by their historical cutoff. All extracted
financial candidates lack verified historical vintage and reviewed reporting
scope. The inspected Light forecast relation has no native dated forecast vintage.

Therefore no raw/display FMI or adjusted forecast can be released. The previous
export presented these reviewed drivers, leaving much of the retrieved source
context in other tabs. It was a coverage/review report, not a finished leaderboard.
Approval flags alone cannot repair missing vintage/scope evidence.
