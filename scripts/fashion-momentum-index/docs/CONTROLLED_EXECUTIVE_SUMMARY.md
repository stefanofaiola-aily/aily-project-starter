# FMI executive summary

- **Coverage and inputs:** represent all prospects; review company/ticker/symbol mappings, brand-versus-group scope, approved peers and collection dates in Excel. Measurements come from Light/shared databases, not manually copied reports.
- **Revenue metrics:** select three comparable revenue observations; calculate earlier/latest YoY growth, approved-peer median growth and brand-versus-peer gaps in percentage points.
- **Instagram metrics:** measure median-likes lift against baseline. Show posts cover days 0–7; baseline pools days −30 to −1 and +8 to +37, with five usable posts per sample. Annual analyses require comparable windows, 20 posts per year and three common months.
- **Raw FMI:** combine 50% peer-relative revenue performance, 30% Instagram lift and 20% change in peer-relative growth. Missing components block the headline; raw score determines High/Medium/Neutral/Low classification.
- **Display score:** rescale reviewed selected headlines to 70–98, rounded half-up to one decimal; equal/single-score cohorts map to 84. Raw classifications remain unchanged, and cohort membership/extrema are recorded.
- **Forecast uplift:** apply a configurable exponential scenario to the rounded display score: approximately 2.22% at 80 and 9.37% at 98. Adjust only positive, scope/currency/unit-compatible forecasts with a verified pre-cutoff vintage and a target after cutoff.
- **App delivery:** provide 167 typed fields covering component scores, revenues, prior periods, growth, peer gaps, trend, social samples and forecast adjustments. Optional comments, views, followers, gross profit, EBITDA and margins do not change FMI weights.
- **Historical controls:** freeze show evidence at the end of local show date +37; exclude late or timezone-unverified captures and undated forecasts. Preserve historical outputs. Cohort changes can alter uplift; the positive-uplift scenario is not calibrated predictive accuracy.
- **Current readiness:** the pipeline and Excel are implemented; 140 tests passed. All 92 prospects are included, but no production FMI or adjusted forecasts are released until required reviews and historical evidence are available.
