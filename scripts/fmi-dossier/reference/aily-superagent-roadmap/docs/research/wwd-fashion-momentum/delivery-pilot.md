## Calculation and evidence ledger

The PR calculates each component as `min(75, max(60, 50 × current/reference))`, averages usable components equally and rounds only the final display half-up. Actual growth is `current/reference − 1`.

**Why the image says 71 and the PR says 69:** the VS raw component mean is approximately **71.1633**, which rounds to **71**; applying the PR’s per-component bounds gives **69.0741**, which rounds to **69**. This reproduces the two values without promoting the candidate or selecting a new method. The image’s “50 = unchanged · 100 = double the reference” describes the raw ratio scale; it must not be copied as the complete explanation of a final score bounded to 60–75.

**Burberry:** `(60 + 68.75) / 2 = 64.375 → 64`. The score does not mean attention grew 64%. The EMV floor masks a decline, so both growth figures and the mixed headline are needed.
