**Yes: these channels can provide useful additional external signals.** This focused review verified six official Instagram posts across J.Crew and ME+EM, ME+EM’s public account size, and two editorial items about J.Crew’s campaign. It is a feasibility pilot, not an all-prospect audit. No new index is assigned.

## Recommended prospect experience

Keep the Fashion Momentum index where the existing evidence supports it. Add a small **public-presence snapshot** to provide useful content when the historical index is missing: current public post response, observed media coverage and an internal-data invitation. Use an observation date and evidence-coverage label. Keep full audit details under the information control.

Suggested fields: **Instagram response**, **editorial coverage in the defined panel**, and optionally **search interest**. Leave unknown fields empty. Follower size supplies context; it does not establish momentum or reached audience. A broader current-presence numeric score would have a different methodology and should be named and explained separately.

## Consistent collection method

### Instagram

1. Verify the official brand account through the brand site or matching profile identity; retain brand, parent and division scope.
2. For event momentum, enumerate all relevant official posts in the same -3/+7-day event window for both editions, record format and date, and compare counters at matched ages when historic captures exist.
3. Without historic captures, label observed current counters as a snapshot. Do not invent seven-day historical counters or compare today’s old-post counters as though they were measured at equal age.
4. For a current account audit, use a predefined chronological sample such as the latest 20 eligible posts in a fixed date range; disclose sample completeness and split Reels from photo/carousel posts. Do not sample only viral posts.
5. Use median likes/comments per post to reduce domination by one celebrity post; report visible video views separately when accessible. A current follower-normalized interaction proxy must name its denominator and is not reach-based engagement.
6. Reach, impressions, saves, shares, website clicks, paid versus organic allocation and attribution are absent from this pilot. Owner Insights or a documented provider would be needed to fill these fields.

### Media coverage

1. Define one publisher panel, language and geography for all brands, and explicit current/reference event windows. A separate calendar-period presence view can use matched 28-day windows, but that changes the comparison from event momentum.
2. Record title, URL, publication date, event relevance, publisher, brand scope and editorial/press-release/shopping category for each item.
3. Deduplicate canonical URLs and syndicated/reprinted stories. Keep company releases and republications separate from editorial coverage; classify affiliate shopping articles visibly.
4. Count verified distinct stories and publishers within the defined panel. Call this observed coverage in the panel, not all internet coverage. Paywalled or inaccessible sources have explicit unknown states.
5. Compare current/reference counts only under the same retrieval and inclusion rules; search-engine estimated result totals are not article counts. Article counts do not establish sentiment, readership or sales.

Proposed initial panel: Vogue, WWD, The Impression, FashionUnited, Harper’s Bazaar, Marie Claire, InStyle, The Fashionisto, Town & Country, The Hollywood Reporter. This is a proposal; the pilot did not exhaustively search this panel.

## Further ideas worth using

- **Campaign response relative to the brand’s usual posts:** Shows whether an event stands out for that brand rather than rewarding the biggest follower base. Requires: Same formats and matched counter ages, complete or clearly defined samples; keep observational and non-causal.
- **Creator amplification:** Separates response on the brand’s own account from attention on creator accounts. Requires: Verified campaign-related creator posts; label overlapping audiences and do not sum followers as unique reach.
- **Brand and hero-product search interest:** Helps distinguish broad brand attention from interest in a specific bag, jacket or dress. Requires: Same Google Trends topic/query, geography, time range and normalization; keep separate from approved score categories.
- **Public product availability:** Makes the invitation tangible: attention around a hero product versus visible sizes/colors available. Requires: Timestamped observations from official shops; a public out-of-stock state does not prove sales or demand.
- **YouTube and TikTok video response:** Extends beyond Instagram where official campaign/show videos are public. Requires: Verified official videos, matched elapsed windows and separate platforms; cumulative views are not unique audience.

## Practical next layer

1. Extend the public-presence snapshot to the 18 brand packages using verified official accounts and a fixed sample. Preserve the shared retailer board.
2. Build the dated article ledger within the same publisher panel for current and reference event windows. Show completeness and inaccessible sources.
3. Promote measurements into the original momentum calculation only when at least two comparable pairs exist. Preserve separate current-only context otherwise.
4. Use the gaps to make the CTA concrete: internal sales/stock data can help investigate whether visible attention aligns with product demand and availability.

This route can improve the usefulness of every teaser without waiting for a full provider export. It cannot recreate historical social capture windows or guarantee complete media coverage from ordinary web search.
